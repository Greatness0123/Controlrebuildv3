# Social Media Links for Control Web

I have provided placeholders for these in the footer and help sections. When you are ready, please update them with your official profiles:

1. **X (formerly Twitter)**: Place your link in `app/page.tsx` and `components/Footer.tsx`.
2. **GitHub**: Add your public repo URL here.
3. **Discord**: Add your community invite link.
4. **LinkedIn**: Company page link.
5. **YouTube**: Tutorial or marketing channel.

Look for the string `"#SOCIAL_LINK_HERE"` in the codebase to find where to update.
